About the game

Say What Now?! is a word-party game where how a word sounds matters more than how it's spelled. Build a word from your consonant cards and the vowels on the table to match the round card, announce your score, then say it out loud and defend it.

If someone doubts your word, they shout "Say What Now?!" and the group decides. No phones, no looking things up. It's quick to learn, and it's even better when you add your own house rules.

  • Sound over spelling. Get creative with how a word is said.
  • Say What Now?! challenges. Defend your word or you're out of the round.
  • Strategic scoring. Cards are worth 1-4 points, and you announce your score before you reveal.
