Insheepnia

Count your sheep. Steal theirs. Fall asleep first.

2-8 players
10-15 mins
Ages 8+
How to play Card guide
How to play Card guide House rules FAQ

How to play

It's 3am and you're wide awake. Everyone has a secret bed card showing how many sheep they need to fall asleep. Be the first to hit your number exactly and you win.

There's no paper rulebook in the box. This page is the rulebook, and we've left room for you to bend the rules and make the game your own. Got a house rule you love? Share it with us.

Setup

  1. Check the moons. Some sheep cards have a moon in the corner. Take out any your group is too small for:
    PlayersTake out
    23+, 5+ and 7+ moon cards
    3-45+ and 7+ moon cards
    5-67+ moon cards
    7-8Nothing, use them all
  2. Shuffle the 8 bed cards and deal one face down to each player. Peek at yours and keep it secret. Put any spare bed cards back in the box without looking.
  3. Shuffle the main deck and put it in the middle. Everyone starts with no cards. You're lying in bed, wide awake.
  4. Whoever went to bed last last night goes first. Play goes clockwise.

Your turn

Do one of these:

  • Draw the top card of the deck.
  • Play one power or modifier card and do what it says.
  • Announce that you're falling asleep (see Winning).

Hand limit: 7 cards. If you have more than 7 at the end of your turn, discard down to 7. Handy if you've got too many sheep!

Warm Milk is the exception: play it any time to block a card that affects you. It doesn't use your turn.

Counting your sheep

  • Only sheep cards count. Black Sheep count as −1. Power and modifier cards count as nothing.
  • Modifier cards stay face up in a player's bed (in front of them). Your sheep needed is your bed number, plus or minus everything in your bed. It can never go below 1.
  • Too many sheep? You're not out, you just can't fall asleep yet. Time to shear some off!

Winning

On your turn, announce and reveal your hand and your bed card.

  • Exactly right? You fall asleep and win. Sweet dreams!
  • Not quite? False alarm! Every other player takes one random card from your hand, and play carries on.

Because announcing uses your turn, if you hit your number you'll have to survive one round of everyone else first. Hide that smile.

Playing with younger kids? Leave out the false alarm penalty.

Card guide

112 cards in the box: 104 in the main deck, plus 8 bed cards.

Sheep cards 62

The sheep you’re counting. Only these count towards your total.

1 Sheep 22

Counts as 1 sheep.

2 Sheep 20

Counts as 2 sheep.

3 Sheep 16

Counts as 3 sheep.

Black Sheep 4

Counts as −1 sheep. On your turn, play it into another player’s hand. It’s stuck there until it’s sheared or stolen.

Power cards 32

Play one on your turn (Warm Milk can be played any time). Power cards don’t count as sheep.

Wolf 5

Take one random card from another player’s hand.

Warm Milk 4

Play any time to block a card that affects you, even ones that hit everyone. Doesn’t use your turn.

Shear 4

Discard one card from your own hand.

Lullaby 3

Draw 2 cards.

Snore 3

The next player skips their turn.

Night Owl 3

Secretly look at another player’s bed card.

Sleepwalker 2

Swap your whole hand with another player’s.

Nightmare 2

Every player passes one random card to the player on their left.

Fire Alarm 2

Every other player discards down to 3 cards.

Duvet Hog 2

Take two random cards from another player’s hand.

Midnight Snack 2

Take any one card from the discard pile.

Modifier cards 10

These stay face up in a player’s bed and change how many sheep they need, until that player clears them with Earplugs.

Double Espresso 3

Another player needs +1 sheep. Put this in their bed.

Insomnia 2

Another player needs +2 sheep. Put this in their bed.

Neighbour’s Dog 1

Every other player needs +1 sheep. Put this in your own bed.

Hot Water Bottle 2

You need −1 sheep. Put this in your bed, or in another player’s.

Earplugs 2

Only works for you. Discard one modifier card from your bed, or keep this in your bed to block out the Neighbour’s Dog.

Bed cards 8

One each, dealt face down. Your secret number.

Bed card: 8 2

You need 8 sheep to fall asleep. Keep it secret!

Bed card: 9 2

You need 9 sheep to fall asleep. Keep it secret!

Bed card: 10 2

You need 10 sheep to fall asleep. Keep it secret!

Bed card: 11 2

You need 11 sheep to fall asleep. Keep it secret!
